## Hope for Dogs Europe photographs

The brief asked the site to review https://www.hopefordogseurope.com/en/honden.html as a photography reference.

- The page was reachable and contains rescue-dog photographs belonging to Hope for Dogs Europe.
- No public image-reuse license or written permission was found.
- Those files were **not downloaded**, **not hotlinked**, and **not used**.
- Dogs named on that site were **not** copied into this project as adoption listings.
