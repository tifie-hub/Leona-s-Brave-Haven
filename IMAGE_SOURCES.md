## Rescue photographs

All dog photographs on the site were supplied by the organization in `dogs images and stories/`. They are stored locally in `public/images/dogs/` and used for Meet the Dogs, rescue stories, and supporting photography.

The homepage header uses `public/images/header.webp`, supplied as `header image.webp`.

Stories were taken from the supplied Word document, edited for the website, and published without social-media icons, Messenger prompts, or sharing links.
